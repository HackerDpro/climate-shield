How to read these numbers

EONET and GDACS entries are reported events. The map's density score counts reports rounded into roughly one-degree areas; it is not a measure of fire size or severity. NASA FIRMS records are satellite heat detections, not confirmed incidents, and are shown separately unless they match a reported event within about 750 metres and 24 hours.

The project hazard index combines local wind, reported-event density and a terrain multiplier. Ignition risk combines current weather and modelled soil moisture. Spread and water figures use simple project heuristics. None of these scores has been validated as an operational fire forecast, damage estimate or evacuation recommendation.

FIRMS radiative power is summed from available satellite detections and is not total carbon emissions.
